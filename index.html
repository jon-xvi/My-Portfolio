JOHN ERIZU

Hi, I'm John, a product designer based in Abuja, Nigeria. For over four years, I've been turning ideas into digital products, figuring out what people need, how things should work, and how to make them easier to use.

I enjoy the messy part of building products. Asking questions, challenging assumptions, exploring ideas, and finding practical solutions to problems worth solving.

My work doesn't stop in Figma. I use AI tools to experiment, write code, and bring my designs to life. It's changed how I work, giving me the freedom to test ideas, build functional prototypes, and turn concepts into working products.

I like being involved from the first idea to the final product. For me, it's not just about making things look good, but understanding what we're building, why it matters, and making sure it actually works.
