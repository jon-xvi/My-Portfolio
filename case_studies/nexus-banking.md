### The Challenge
Traditional banking apps are cluttered with legacy features, confusing navigation, and high cognitive load. For Nexus, we aimed to create a streamlined, modern fintech interface that makes daily financial tasks feel effortless and visually satisfying.

![Nexus Banking Redesign Screen](artifacts/projects/project_standard_1781015903362.png)

### The Process
We began by mapping out user journeys for the most common tasks: checking balances, sending money, and paying bills. Through iterative user testing, we stripped away secondary clutter and created a simplified navigation paradigm built around a dynamic dashboard.

### Key Design Decisions
- **Minimal Friction Flows:** Reduced the transfer flow from 5 steps to just 2 steps.
- **Sensory Feedback:** Integrated micro-animations and haptic cues to build confidence during transactions.
- **Elevated Dark Mode:** Crafted a custom high-contrast dark palette to reduce eye strain.
